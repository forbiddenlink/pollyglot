Overview

PollyGlot is built to provide translation features with minimal data collection. This page explains what data may be processed, why it is used, and how long it is retained.

Data processed for translation

When you request a translation, the text you submit and selected language settings are sent to the translation API provider in order to generate results. This processing is required to provide core functionality. Translation requests may include temporary metadata such as request time and IP-derived rate-limit counters for abuse protection.

Services that process your data

These services receive data when you use the matching feature. Some run only when the site operator has turned them on.

  • OpenAI: receives the text you submit to translate it, detect its language, and create pronunciation guides and alternative phrasings. It can also turn text into speech.
  • Magica (ElevenLabs voices): receives text to generate speech, and receives recorded audio to transcribe it when your browser has no built-in speech recognition.
  • MiniMax: receives text to generate speech.
  • Langfuse: records the prompts and results of translation requests for debugging and quality monitoring.
  • PostHog: records product analytics events with the language pair, tone, text length, and your IP address.
  • Upstash: stores your IP address in rate-limit counters for abuse protection.
  • Vercel: hosts the site and the API, and handles your requests.
  • Google Fonts: your browser requests fonts from Google when a page loads.
  • jsDelivr and Hugging Face: when you use pronunciation practice, your browser loads speech recognition and waveform libraries from jsDelivr and downloads the speech model from Hugging Face. The recording is processed in your browser.

Local storage and settings

PollyGlot stores settings, translation history, and favorites in your browser local storage so your preferences persist between sessions. You can clear this data at any time from your browser or by using in-app controls. Local storage data is not shared with third parties by default.

Security and retention

The service applies input validation and request throttling to reduce abuse risk. Server logs may contain operational error information for debugging and reliability. We aim to keep retained operational data limited to what is necessary for security and uptime.
