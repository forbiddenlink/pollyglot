## Synthesis

Combine Exemplar/Tengile's editorial typography, Tekt's material restraint, Mintlify's practical reading hierarchy and Terms' input-first composition. Keep PollyGlot's established green/orange character. Use typographic multilingual details instead of stock travel photography: the product is about words, and an image-led hero would delay the main task. Avoid cinematic preloaders, scroll hijacking and gratuitous 3D.
