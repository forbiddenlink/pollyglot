About PollyGlot

PollyGlot is an AI-assisted translation app designed for practical everyday use. The goal is simple: keep translation fast, useful, and easy to trust. Instead of hiding core controls behind complicated workflows, PollyGlot keeps language selection, tone controls, translation history, and speech features visible in one interface.

What the app focuses on

The app supports 20 languages and prioritizes clear output with minimal noise. For short phrases, PollyGlot can offer alternatives so users can choose the wording that best fits context. For non-Latin scripts, pronunciation guidance is included to make output more usable in real conversations. Voice input and text-to-speech are built in for faster hands-free use.

How translation works

PollyGlot sends text to an AI translation service and returns only the translated result. Requests are validated and rate-limited to reduce abuse and keep response quality stable. No account creation is required for basic use, and users can manage local history settings, including save behavior and storage limits, directly in the app.

PollyGlot is built and maintained by Elizabeth Stein.
